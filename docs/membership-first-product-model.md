# Membership-First Product Model

Localized.life should become a membership platform instead of a loose public directory. Membership is the trust layer that lets the site protect private details, verify local information, give owners control, and create real revenue.

## Core Shift

Old model:

- Public listings first.
- Claim/edit tools later.
- Research items can accidentally feel public too early.
- Revenue is unclear until traffic grows.

New model:

- Membership and claimed profiles are the center.
- Public pages show safe summaries and area-level information.
- Private details live behind member/admin tools.
- Regional managers verify local fit before listings go public.
- Revenue starts with useful account features, profile tools, sponsorships, and manager-supported onboarding.

## Member Types

### Free Community Member

For shoppers, neighbors, and people who want to save or report local information.

Possible tools:

- Save sales, events, profiles, mentors, and services.
- Build routes.
- Report incorrect information.
- Follow counties or towns.
- Request services or local goods.

### Listing Owner Member

For people who own a sale, market profile, mentor profile, event, service listing, harvest site, or local project.

Possible tools:

- Claim and manage public profile.
- Choose public contact and pickup/service details.
- Hide exact address unless they decide otherwise.
- Add products, dates, photos, availability, and seasonal notes.
- Pause, archive, or remove listings.

### Regional Manager Member

For trusted people who manage a county or region.

Possible tools:

- Verify submitted and sourced listings.
- Mark items as lead, needs verification, unclaimed, claimed, hidden, or archived.
- Help owners claim profiles.
- Review reports from the public.
- Maintain local quality and remove wrong-state or stale listings.

### Sponsor/Partner Member

For local sponsors, print partners, organizers, community groups, estate sale companies, farmers markets, and regional partners.

Possible tools:

- Sponsor a town, county, category, route, or event page.
- Submit official events and community sale hubs.
- Manage partner profile and campaign details.

## Initial Membership Tiers

### Free

- Save and follow local items.
- Submit reports.
- Basic claim request.
- Basic public profile after verification.

### Local Seller / Mentor

For Local Market, Local Mentors, Local Services, and small local profile owners.

Possible price:

- Low monthly subscription or annual plan.
- Optional free tier during early launch.

Benefits:

- Manage claimed profile.
- Add multiple products or offerings.
- Add photos.
- Set availability.
- Protect private address/contact details.
- Get a cleaner public page.

### Event / Sale Organizer

For garage sale organizers, city-wide sale hosts, vendor markets, workshops, and community events.

Benefits:

- Manage event/sale pages.
- Add photos, flyers, schedules, links, maps, or vendor lists.
- Create share pages.
- Attach sale stops to an event.
- Promote updates.

### Regional Manager

Usually invite-only at first.

Benefits:

- Manager dashboard.
- Assigned counties.
- Verification queue.
- Revenue share or pay rules.

## Revenue Ideas

- Paid claimed profiles for sellers, mentors, services, and organizers.
- Featured profile or sponsored category placements with clear labeling.
- County/town sponsorships.
- Manager-assisted onboarding fee.
- Event organizer tools and promoted event pages.
- Local services request/matching fee after trust is established.
- Print/flyer partner packages for garage sales, events, and local sellers.

## Regional Manager Pay Ideas

- Revenue share from paid profiles in their assigned area.
- Flat payment per verified active listing.
- Bonus for owner claims completed.
- Commission on sponsors or paid onboarding.
- Hybrid model: small base plus performance pay when a region earns revenue.

## Product Rules

- Researched individual listings should start private.
- Exact addresses should not be public by default.
- Full personal names should be protected when possible until the owner claims the profile.
- Public pages can show area, product/service summary, category, and claim/report buttons.
- Owners choose what contact, pickup, and location details become public.
- Regional managers verify location and fit before publishing sourced listings.
