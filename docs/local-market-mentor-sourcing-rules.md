# Local Market And Mentor Sourcing Rules

Local Market and Local Mentors should prioritize individual people, households, gardens, small homestead setups, and informal teachers in the nearby area.

Local Market items with enough public detail can become public listings. Local Mentor items found from outside sources should stay private as outreach leads until the person agrees to be listed and the lesson format, service area, and contact path are clear.

## Good Fits

- Backyard eggs, honey, produce, flowers, starts, plants, sourdough, cottage foods, preserves, compost, firewood, and handmade useful goods.
- Music lessons, tutoring, art lessons, garden lessons, canning help, farm/homestead skills, repair coaching, computer basics, and practical one-on-one learning.
- Posts from individuals, families, neighbors, hobby growers, backyard makers, and community members.
- Area-based posts in Will County, Cook County, Kankakee County, Grundy County, Kendall County, Lake County IN, Porter County IN, Jasper County IN, and Newton County IN.

## Required Before Publishing

- A city, neighborhood, county, or pickup/service area.
- A public contact path, source link, profile/page link, email/phone shown publicly, or clear way for a customer to reach the person.
- Enough detail to explain what is offered.
- Admin notes showing where the lead came from and what still needs review.

## Exclude

- Registered businesses, LLCs, storefronts, franchises, agencies, large farms, commercial schools, and government/business directories.
- Generic business listings without an individual person behind the offer.
- Posts with no location, no contact path, or no reviewable source.
- Private Facebook group content that cannot be reviewed publicly.

## Facebook Use

Facebook is a lead source only. Use public posts, public profiles/pages, or user-provided screenshots/links when they show enough detail. Do not scrape private groups, private profiles, Marketplace, or logged-in-only content. Treat mentor-related Facebook finds as private outreach leads first, not public listings.
