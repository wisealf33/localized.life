# Regional Manager Operating Model

Localized.life should not rely on unverified search results becoming public listings. Local Market, Local Mentors, Local Events, and SaleTrail need a review path where a local person confirms that listings belong in the claimed area before they appear publicly.

## Why This Matters

- Search and social results can look local but actually belong to another state or region.
- Individual sellers may share exact addresses, full names, or pickup details that should not be republished without care.
- Localized.life needs trust: shoppers should believe the listing is nearby, current, and reachable.
- The site can grow faster if trusted regional managers help verify and maintain specific counties.

## Suggested Roles

### Regional Manager

Owns a county or small region, such as Will County, Kankakee County, Cook County, or Northwest Indiana.

Responsibilities:

- Review new public submissions.
- Verify that sourced listings are actually local.
- Contact owners before publishing sensitive individual listings.
- Approve, hide, correct, or archive listings.
- Help onboard local sellers, mentors, event organizers, and community sale hosts.
- Watch for stale listings and wrong information.

### Listing Owner

The person who owns a product profile, mentor profile, event, service, or sale listing.

Responsibilities:

- Claim their profile or listing.
- Choose what public contact details should show.
- Keep products, dates, prices, and pickup/service areas current.
- Report when they are paused, sold out, seasonal, or no longer offering something.

### Platform Admin

Owns the broader system and manager standards.

Responsibilities:

- Approve regional managers.
- Set verification rules.
- Review disputes, privacy issues, and removals.
- Build monetization and payout rules.
- Keep the public trust layer consistent across all regions.

## Listing States

- Lead: private item found through research or submitted with incomplete details.
- Needs verification: possible public listing, but location/contact/owner status is not confirmed.
- Unclaimed public profile: safe public area-only listing with protected owner details.
- Claimed public profile: owner has confirmed and can maintain details.
- Hidden: not shown publicly because it is wrong, stale, private, or outside the region.
- Archived: kept for history but no longer active.

## Verification Rules

Before a researched individual listing goes public:

- Confirm it is in the right county or service area.
- Confirm it is an individual, household, community seller, or informal mentor when that section requires it.
- Avoid publishing exact home addresses unless the owner explicitly wants that public.
- Protect full personal names when possible until the owner claims the profile.
- Prefer a claim/contact path over exposing private pickup details.
- If the source is uncertain, keep it as a lead.

## Onboarding Flow

1. A person submits a listing or a regional manager finds a possible listing.
2. The item enters a private lead/review queue.
3. Regional manager verifies local fit and sensitivity.
4. If safe, create an unclaimed public profile with area-only details.
5. Owner claims profile and chooses what to show publicly.
6. Owner can add products, dates, availability, photos, and pickup/contact rules.
7. Regional manager periodically checks stale or reported listings.

## Possible Revenue

- Claimed profile upgrades for sellers, mentors, service providers, and event organizers.
- Regional sponsorships for county pages or category pages.
- Featured listings with clear labeling.
- Manager-assisted onboarding packages.
- Small local seller subscription for multiple products, photos, and seasonal updates.
- Event organizer tools for maps, routes, vendor lists, and promoted community pages.
- Service request fees or lead fees where appropriate, but only after trust and consent are clear.

## Possible Regional Manager Pay

- Revenue share by region.
- Flat amount per verified/maintained active listing.
- Commission on claimed profile upgrades from their region.
- Bonus for sponsor sales or manager-assisted onboarding.
- Hybrid model: small base plus performance incentives.

## Near-Term Product Changes

- Add private status fields for Local Market and Local Mentor research items.
- Add admin filters for lead, needs verification, unclaimed, claimed, hidden, and archived.
- Add owner claim flow for Local Market profiles.
- Add private address/contact fields stored outside public source files.
- Add manager assignment by county or region.
- Add public report button to every profile and listing.
- Do not publish researched individual listings directly without regional verification.
